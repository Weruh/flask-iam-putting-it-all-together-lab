# Reciplease — the shared kitchen

Audience: home cooks looking for everyday inspiration. Primary actions: discover a recipe and pass on a favorite.

Creative thesis: a community cookbook brought to the screen, with warm paper, expressive serif headlines, ingredient-inspired colors and quiet editorial rules. The signature is the juxtaposition of oversized italic recipe language, authentic food photography, and numbered cookbook entries.

Palette: paper #f7f4ec, forest #203c31, tomato #ae402c, pale sage #e9ecde. Type: Georgia headlines and DM Sans UI, with system fallbacks. Generous desktop gutters collapse into a single mobile column; forms always retain visible labels. Motion uses short hover transitions and honors reduced motion.

References consulted: Awwwards Food & Drink gallery (https://www.awwwards.com/websites/food-drink/) for food-led composition; Dribbble cookbook collection (https://dribbble.com/search/cookbook) for printed cookbook typography and numbered entries. Mobbin's food category redirected to general marketing content, so no specific flow was adopted. Reference content was retrieved as text and image descriptions, not visually reviewed as live sites.
